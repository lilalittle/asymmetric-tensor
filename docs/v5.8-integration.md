## Author changes and migration

The immutable v5.7-to-v5.8 comparison has 466→473 bounded named declarations,
seven added, none removed and one changed; declaration docstrings increase
74→75. Both structures, all three explicit instance bodies and source import/
section-context directives are unchanged. Scanner totals do not cover all
compiled generated APIs.

- `raiseBoth_eta_entries` and `raised_contravariant_divergence_bridge` add selected
  eta-raising signs, comoving entries and spatial derivative equalities.
- `lie_derivative_boost_residual_bridge` derives the local boost residual from
  explicit metric/Jacobian data and gives its positive-admittance vacuum
  consequence when the displayed Lie component vanishes.
- `frobenius_vs_lorentzian_shear_bridge` gives the factor-of-two normalization and
  positive Frobenius sum versus Lorentzian difference.
- `counterbalanced_cavitation_regularity` derives strict interior/nonzero bounds
  under its displayed load/background hypotheses and separates inverse-power
  cancellation from a denominator-free boundary-polynomial equivalence.
- `leviCivitaConnection` is a coordinate Christoffel expression;
  `independent_flat_connection_divergence_bridge` derives pointwise zero
  connection and divergence reduction under explicit premises.
- `topological_phase_lock_cavitation` changes from four to five conjuncts.
  All-entry `U_anti` independence is the new second conjunct. The tensor arguments
  retain symbolic `alpha_s` under `alpha_s = 0`; older projection consumers must
  migrate deliberately. `V58Migration.lean` reconstructs the old four-result view
  and exercises the new conjunct at distinct nonunit admittances.

The v5.7 dimensional basis, inhabited on-shell conservation, explicit four-vector
eigenpair bridge and total/pressure-only optical distinction remain unchanged.
The source upload, declarations, proof tokens, docstrings and dependency pins
are not silently repaired or replaced with a different reference.

these source-pinned qualifications accepted by the independent source review:
