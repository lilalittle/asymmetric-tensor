How the proposal handles it:
- `issues-05`: a reopened record can later close when new current-source evidence meets its unchanged obligation. Neither the older closure nor the older failure is erased.

- The optional rescaling is not promoted into a newly asserted physical force. This closure does not validate the force model empirically.
- Old helper names or broken historical documentation links can produce a separate docs gap. They do not justify reopening a now-satisfied mathematical formula criterion by association.

If a later revision drops the unscaled baseline again, reuse/reopen #27 with the new evidence. Do not create another “extra coupling” issue just because the commit or displayed equation number changed.
